Model and examples · the model, 6 minutes

The chain works in two directions

Downward

From the goal to the metric, to the technical decision

Upward

From one technical detail up to the effect that someone in the company will see

If a detail cannot answer “who will see what”, it is not connected yet. When time is short, cut the scope, not the quality.

Four concepts, one question each

Unit economics

“How much value does one unit of work have, and how much does it cost?”

Moat

“What makes this benefit hard to remove or redo?”

Runway

“How long can this change keep running before it runs out of what it needs?”
